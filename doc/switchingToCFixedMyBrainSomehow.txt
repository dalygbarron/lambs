You would think that C is the most annoying language to do a project like this
in, you need to deal with a whole bunch of data structures and they interact
a lot. It's not even like a game where you can get around to writing a hashmap
implementation for something eventually... you need all this stuff immediately
just for the program to do anything at all.

However, a check of the git history will show a python version, a go version,
there was a C++ version... I kept trying to design this damn datastructure and
I just couldn't do it. Admittedly this suggests I am a simpleton, but
regardless, the moment I gave it a little go in C suddenly I could pull it off.

I think the reason is that the trouble I was having was that I had ideas but
they were vague and questions would assail my mind and pull it apart from all
angles. When you are writing in C and dealing with all that "low level bullshit"
it means that you have to build your system up all the way up from the
foundations as a collection of well defined pieces of functionality, and because
of that, the more you write, the more resistant to vagueness your work
becomes.
